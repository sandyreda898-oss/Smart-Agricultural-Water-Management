## 5. Key Takeaways for Phase 4 (Feature Engineering & Splitting)
1. **Group Splitting Mandatory:** Split by `weather_cell_id`; only 5 independent weather series are present, so CV will be exploratory and have high uncertainty.
2. **Retrospective features only:** full-season weather summaries are unavailable at planting time; define forecast/climatology/partial-season inputs before building a live prediction interface.
3. **Soil Exclusions:** 2 grid points lack soil profiles (`exclude_from_ml=True`) and must remain excluded or imputed strictly inside training folds.
4. **No Measured Farm Target:** Supervised model training remains blocked until field survey irrigation labels (`m3/feddan`) are collected.
